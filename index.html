Straight answers

What is IDX, and do I need it?
IDX is the feed that puts live MLS listings on your site, so buyers search homes with you instead of on Zillow. It needs your broker's approval and an IDX Broker subscription, which they bill you for directly. If you just want a sharp site with your own featured listings, the Agent Site covers that.
How does the AI quote assistant work?
It's a short chat on your site that asks what your estimator would: the address, the job, the timeline, whether insurance is involved. The lead gets sent to you with the answers already filled in. Try it at the bottom of any of the demo sites.
Do I own the site?
Yes. Your domain, your site. If we ever part ways, I hand it over. No hostage situations.
What do you need from me?
Your headshot and a few photos, your logo if you have one, the cities you work, and answers to ten quick questions by text. That's it.

How does paying work?
